# Sprint 123: Customer invoice issuance transition

A master organization's finance manager may approve one calculation-backed customer invoice draft through `POST /api/v1/customer-invoices/{uuid}/issue`. The command requires a UUID idempotency key, unique document number within the issuer, issue date, due date, taxable-supply date for a payer invoice and an audit reason. It verifies complete issuer and customer identity and address, the issuer VAT number for standard VAT, the draft status and exact sum of lines. The transaction creates one receivable commercial identity with an immutable party, amount and line snapshot, stores a SHA-256 evidence digest in its audit event, and marks the billing document approved. Retries with the same key and content return the same identity; reuse with different content is rejected.

The customer may read approved and closed invoices through the existing scoped detail endpoint. The original calculation sources and draft snapshots remain recorded. Payment, ledger posting, PDF rendering and external sending are separate workflows. Operational deployment should review Czech tax document requirements and the organization's billing policy before using this transition for real invoices.
