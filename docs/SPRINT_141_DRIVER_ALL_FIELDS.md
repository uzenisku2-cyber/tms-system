# Sprint 141 – Driver correction of imported reports

The driver may correct every field of their own imported draft, including route number, date, surcharge and configured custom values. Any disagreement with depot records is resolved in review before billing.

The record stays owned by the master organization and retains its import provenance. Both the old and new service dates must remain within the driver's carrier assignment and the carrier's relationship with the master. Optimistic versioning and audit events remain required.
